## UC-2 — Implement en passant capture

**Beschreibung**

As a player, I want pawns to be able to capture en passant according to FIDE rules, so that all legal pawn moves are available. En passant applies when an opponent pawn advances two squares from its starting rank and lands beside one of my pawns; my pawn may capture it as if it had advanced only one square. The right exists only on the immediately following move. Affected modules: `src/chess/domain/rules.py` (move generation), `src/chess/domain/game_state.py` (en passant target state), `src/chess/application/game.py` (move execution and target lifecycle).

**Akzeptanzkriterien**

1. Given an opponent pawn has just advanced two squares and landed adjacent to one of my pawns, when legal moves are generated, then the en passant capture is included.
2. Given the en passant capture is played, when the move completes, then the captured pawn is removed from its actual square and not from the capturing pawn target square.
3. Given one full move passes without the en passant capture being played, when legal moves are generated, then the en passant capture is no longer available.
4. Given any two-square pawn advance, when the game state is updated, then the en passant target square is set correctly and cleared after the next move if unused.
5. Unit tests cover a valid en passant capture, expiry after one move, en passant target handling in game state, and at least one position proving the legal move list contains the expected en passant move.

## UC-3 — Implement full castling legality checks

**Beschreibung**

As a player, I want castling to be offered only when it is legal per FIDE rules, so that illegal castling moves are impossible. Castling requires that the king and the involved rook have never moved, all squares between them are empty, the king is not currently in check, and the king does not pass through or land on an attacked square. Affected modules: `src/chess/domain/rules.py`, `src/chess/domain/game_state.py` (castling rights in state), and `src/chess/application/game.py` (rights revocation and rook relocation on execution).

**Akzeptanzkriterien**

1. Given king and rook are unmoved and all conditions hold, when legal moves are generated, then the corresponding castling move is included for all four castle types.
2. Given the king is in check, would pass through an attacked square, or would land on an attacked square, when legal moves are generated, then castling is not included.
3. Given any square between king and rook is occupied, when legal moves are generated, then castling is not included.
4. Given the king moves, or a rook moves or is captured from its home square, when the game state is updated, then the corresponding castling rights are permanently revoked.
5. Given castling is executed, when the move completes, then king and rook are placed on their correct target squares as a single move.
6. Unit tests cover all four castle types, each blocking condition, and rights revocation after king moves, rook moves, and rook captures on home squares.

## UC-5 — Detect checkmate and stalemate

**Beschreibung**

As a player, I want the game to detect checkmate and stalemate automatically, so that games end with the correct result per FIDE rules. Checkmate means the side to move is in check and has no legal move. Stalemate means the side to move is not in check and has no legal move. Affected modules: `src/chess/application/game.py` (game-state evaluation after each move), `src/chess/domain/rules.py` (legal-move filtering including pinned pieces), and `src/chess/presentation/console.py` (result display).

**Akzeptanzkriterien**

1. Given the side to move is in check and has no legal move, when the position is evaluated after a move, then the game result is set to `CHECKMATE` semantics through the existing winner result values and no further moves are accepted.

## UC-6 — Detect draw by threefold repetition

**Beschreibung**

As a player, I want the game to detect when the same position occurs three times, so that the game is declared a draw per FIDE rules. Two positions count as identical only if piece placement, side to move, castling rights, and the en passant target square all match. The repetitions need not be consecutive. Affected modules: `src/chess/domain/board.py` and `src/chess/domain/game_state.py` (position identity inputs), `src/chess/application/game.py` (repetition counter and result handling), and `src/chess/presentation/console.py` (result display).

**Akzeptanzkriterien**

5. Given the draw is triggered, when the board is rendered, then the UI displays a threefold repetition draw message.
